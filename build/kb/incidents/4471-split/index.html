How to tell them apart

Read the body, not the subject or the intake tag. Intake guesses from the reporter's browser; the cause depends on the tenant, its booking form and the channel (causes.ts).

  • parser: day and month swapped ("7 April went out on 4 July"). The shared parser reads every date month first, and every tenant on file writes the day first. Comes in by app, email, or a booking-v2 form.
  • hint: the same swap, but only through the booking form of a booking-v3 tenant. That form prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field, people type what it says, and the form then reads the date day first. Fixing the parser does not change the hint.
  • timezone: nothing swapped. The date lands one day early or late ("Tuesday 12 May, calendar says Monday 11 May"), an invite arrives before the booking, or one meeting falls on different days in Lisbon and Toronto. The desk already read these as one-day moves (desk-notes.json). The desk counts them under parser, where they are filed.
